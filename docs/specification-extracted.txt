01 | Executive summary
Build a practical billing, product catalog, stock, customer-credit, purchases and business analytics app for small merchants. The primary objective is to let a shop owner issue an accurate invoice in under one minute, see real-time stock levels, collect money, and understand profit/cash exposure without spreadsheets.
Product outcomes
Fast checkout: search or scan item, add quantity/discount, select customer, collect payment, issue PDF invoice.
Accurate inventory: movement-based ledger, purchase receipts, returns, wastage, stock adjustments, low-stock alerts.
Reliable bookkeeping: payments and allocation to invoices; customer ledger; dues and partial settlement.
Merchant clarity: daily sales, collected cash, pending receivables, profit approximation, inventory valuation.
Multi-user control: owners, managers, cashiers and accountants; strict separation among merchants.
Simple onboarding: business details, GST regime, invoice series, one shop, first products and customers.

02 | Business requirements document (BRD)
Problem statement
Small shops frequently use paper registers or disconnected mobile tools. Problems include duplicate invoice numbers, uncertain customer dues, missed purchase entries, stock drift, inconsistent discounts, GST mistakes, and low visibility into margins. The solution is one secure and simple daily-operating tool.
Business objectives
BR-01: Digitize sales and purchase bookkeeping.
BR-02: Reduce stock-outs and excess inventory.
BR-03: Increase cash collection through dues visibility and respectful reminders.
BR-04: Enable compliant-looking invoice records with tax data and audit trail.
BR-05: Support one business to manage multiple outlets (later/optional).
BR-06: Generate recurring SaaS revenue with reasonable usage tiers.
BR-07: Work on typical Android devices with limited bandwidth.
BR-08: Protect customer and merchant financial information.

04 | Core workflows and business rules
Flow A | Organization setup
Sign up / verify identity through Supabase Auth.
Create organization and owner membership in one protected server transaction.
Collect shop name, business address, state, timezone, tax regime (unregistered / regular / composition), optional GSTIN.
Create default outlet, INR currency, invoice prefix and first financial-year sequence.
Add first product/service or import CSV; optionally add customers.
Present demo invoice preview, then enter dashboard.
Flow B | Finalize sales invoice
Cashier opens New Sale; UI fetches eligible products, prices and available stock.
Select customer or Guest/Walk-in. Add product/service lines, quantity, unit and discounts.
Server revalidates pricing, stock, outlet, permissions, customer state, tax regime and tax rules.
Server uses a single database transaction to allocate a unique invoice number, calculate totals, insert lines/taxes/ledger, decrement inventory through movements, and commit.
Generate a versioned PDF snapshot; place in private storage, return signed download link on access.
Record payment(s) or due amount separately; invoice status derives from allocated payments.
Queue event for in-app notification and optionally authorized customer delivery channel.
Flow C | Receive purchased stock
Choose supplier, receive bill number/date and purchase details.
Enter products, quantities, purchase rate and applicable input tax.
On receipt posting, create immutable stock-in movement and update stock balance in one transaction.
Record partial/full payment to supplier; post due balance in supplier ledger.
Recompute weighted average purchase cost if this inventory valuation method is chosen.
Update low-stock views and purchase reports.
Flow D | Credit and collection
Issue invoice with total_due > 0; customer receivable recorded at invoice issue.
Customer profile lists open invoices and available unapplied advances.
Collect cash/UPI/bank; store payment reference and method.
Allocate payment to one or more invoices atomically; never allow over-allocation.
Update balances, send staff receipt confirmation, and retain immutable payment audit.
Manual reminders first; automated reminders require opt-in, channel integration and rate limits.
Business rules, decisions and state machines
Rule ID
Behavior
RULE-01
Every table row is scoped by organization_id; outlet_id applies where relevant.
RULE-02
Invoice number unique per organization + document series + financial year; reserve only inside finalized transaction.
RULE-03
Draft documents may change; finalized sales invoices cannot be silently edited or deleted.
RULE-04
Cancel/credit note or return uses reversal / linked credit transaction with required reason and author.
RULE-05
Money represented as numeric(18,2), quantities numeric(18,3) or scale per UOM; no floating-point tax math.
RULE-06
Tax rate, HSN/SAC and business/customer tax snapshots stored on issued invoice.
RULE-07
Insufficient stock: block sales by default; owner may explicitly enable negative stock with audit warning.
RULE-08
Payment amount > 0; allocated <= payment; invoice paid cannot exceed balance.
RULE-09
Product deactivation preserves historical invoice lines and movements.
RULE-10
All mutation APIs support idempotency_key for retry-sensitive commands.
RULE-11
GST mode determines tax invoice vs bill of supply vs ordinary commercial invoice; never infer compliance solely from entering GSTIN.
RULE-12
Post-event notifications are queued only after successful commit; no duplicate dispatch per event+recipient+channel.
RULE-13
Subscription expiry degrades to read-only / restricted new billing according to written terms; merchant exports preserved.
RULE-14
Guest checkout may omit customer PII when law and invoice type permit; collect mandatory recipient fields when triggered.
Document states
Sales invoice: draft -> issued -> partially_paid / paid; issued may have return/credit-note references; voiding is a separate controlled action, not hard deletion. Purchase: draft -> received -> partly_paid / paid; payments may change settlement but not inventory receipt. Stock count: draft -> submitted -> approved -> posted. Notification: queued -> sending -> delivered_to_provider / failed / retry; FCM acceptance is not proof user saw a message.
05 | Functional requirements (FRD)
Authentication and organization

Supabase Auth session management, email/OTP options chosen per final identity policy.
Acceptance: Invalid credentials receive generic errors; revoked sessions cannot fetch company data.

First owner creates legal business profile, address, GST setting, numbering series and outlet.

Database indexes and integrity constraints
Foreign keys must include organization_id consistency; prefer composite foreign keys or explicit trigger checks where a child references a parent.
Indexes: sales_invoices(org_id, invoice_date desc), (org_id, customer_id, status), products(org_id, lower(name)), stock_movements(org_id, outlet_id, product_id, created_at), notifications(recipient_user_id, read_at).
Unique indexes: organization_members(org_id,user_id), products(org_id,sku) WHERE sku IS NOT NULL, sales_invoices(org_id,invoice_number), device_registrations(user_id, installation_id).
CHECK: quantities > 0 on invoice/purchase lines; amounts >= 0; status in controlled enum or validated lookup; payment allocation <= unallocated amount in transaction.
Soft archive for catalog/contacts; hard delete prohibited for issued transactional documents; audit retention per legal/business policy.
Trigger-based balance maintenance inside SQL transaction; do not update stock_balances directly from Flutter.

10 | GST-aware invoicing and calculation
Compliance distinction and document types
The app will produce structured GST-aware tax invoices or bills of supply when appropriate, but it does NOT automatically produce government-registered electronic invoices (IRN / signed QR). Applicable eligible taxpayers must separately register relevant invoices through an authorized IRP workflow. In the referenced official notification, the e-invoicing mandate applies from INR 5 crore annual aggregate turnover for applicable taxpayers/transactions (effective 1 August 2023); published IRP guidance also states a 30-day reporting limit from 1 April 2025 for AATO INR 10 crore or more. Verify current notifications and merchant eligibility before activating any compliance claim.
A GST professional should verify unregistered merchant invoices, composition scheme, reverse charge, exempt supplies, delivery state, mixed tax rates, intra- vs interstate supplies, refunds and required wording before launch.
Invoice legal/tax information checklist
Supplier legal name, address, GSTIN when registered; unique serial number (Rule 46 constraints), issue date and FY.
Buyer name/address, GSTIN/UIN when required; delivery address and place of supply/state code.
HSN/SAC, description, unit, quantity, taxable value, discount, per-rate taxes and cess as applicable.
Tax split: CGST + SGST/UTGST vs IGST as determined by place-of-supply rules, not just customer phone state.
Grand total, amount paid, balance due, reverse-charge indication where applicable, signature/digital signature requirements as reviewed.
For composition/exempt supplies, issue appropriate bill of supply rather than charging GST as a regular tax invoice.
If actual IRP integration is enabled later: retain IRN, acknowledgement time, signed payload and signed QR returned by authorized IRP; distinguish from ordinary payment QR.
Deterministic math: one line worked example
Illustration only: same-state taxable sale of 2 units at INR 500 each, 10% line discount, exclusive GST 18%. Gross = 2 × 500 = 1,000. Discount = 100. Taxable = 900. CGST 9% = 81; SGST 9% = 81; total tax = 162; line total = INR 1,062. For an applicable interstate supply, the example would normally allocate 18% IGST = INR 162 rather than CGST/SGST. Real tax classification and current rates must come from approved configuration.
Calculation engine contract
Compute from immutable decimal inputs on the server with documented rounding mode and per-line versus invoice-level calculation policy.
Support price inclusive/exclusive of GST, line-level discount and overall discount apportionment.
Rate lookup keyed by tax category, effective date, supply type, and merchant regime; retain original applied rate on invoice lines.
Return tax_breakdown by CGST / SGST / IGST / cess and taxable base per slab; avoid floating-point surprises.
For refunds / credit notes, derive original tax context and line caps; do not apply today's changed rates to historical reversals.
Provide an accountant-facing mismatch/reconciliation view and export; do not label exports as GST returns filed.

Threat model and mitigation
Cross-tenant data leak: RLS + explicit grants + org membership checks + tests with two organizations.

Financial fraud/double charge: unique external references, signed gateway webhooks, audit and idempotency.

SQL / API input attacks: parameterized queries, typed validation, schema-level constraints.
Lost device: session revocation, short-lived signed URLs, device deregistration.
Security QA must-test scenarios
Login as Org A cashier; request Org B invoice by guessed UUID -> 404 or 403, never data.
Org A accountant modifies own role to owner -> rejected.
Same invoice issue command sent 3x concurrently -> one invoice number, one stock decrease.
Expired subscription cannot bypass plan limit by calling API directly.
Client modifies invoice tax total to zero -> server ignores/overrides and recalculates.
Webhook signature invalid -> no payment state change.
Logged out / removed member receives deep link -> must re-auth and be denied.

Suggested engineering decisions
Riverpod for state; go_router for navigation; Freezed/json_serializable optionally for typed request/response models.
Repository/use-case boundaries: UI -> controller/provider -> domain use case -> repository -> Supabase API.
Use a single currency formatting utility and decimal-safe server totals.
Separate dev/stage/prod Supabase projects and Firebase app configurations.
Use GitHub Actions or similar CI for formatting, analyze, unit tests, SQL migration tests, build artifacts.
Start Android-first for merchant pilot; keep iOS platform code ready and test printer/permissions separately.
Pilot merchant / business model validation
Interview 10–20 shops: current invoice frequency, stock record method, credit sales, GST registration, printer models, monthly willingness to pay.
Pilot 2–3 distinct merchant types before adding advanced modules.
Track active invoices per shop, returns/errors, onboarding abandonment, report demand, retention and service costs.
Subscription price examples earlier are hypotheses; adjust using actual willingness-to-pay and Supabase/FCM/PDF/AI/support costs.
Build onboarding, tutorial data and CSV imports so switching from a register or spreadsheet is practical.
Operational runbook checklist
Provision separate environments and secure secret storage; rotate keys, restrict dashboard access.
Monitor Supabase quotas, database CPU, storage, slow SQL, Edge Function errors and audit anomalies.
Configure FCM sender credentials server-side, token hygiene, push opt-out and dispatch queue retries.
Publish privacy policy, support contact, deletion policy, subscription terms and merchant data export method.
Set up backup/restore testing, invoice numbering retention, business continuity and support escalation.
Store release builds with version numbers, test payments/exports in sandbox and production verification plan.
